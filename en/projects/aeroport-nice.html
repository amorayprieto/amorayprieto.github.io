Why Nice Airport's online bookings drop off

Purchase journey audit, data analysis, friction points, A/B test plan and SEO fixes.

  • 73 %drop-off after add-to-cart identified
  • 275internal links without anchor text found
  • 8 100monthly searches on the priority keyword

Context

Nice Côte d'Azur Airport sells services online: parking, fast track, VIP lounge, baggage protection, a loyalty programme. Working from a real dataset, the brief was to find where visitors drop off and propose ways to raise conversion and qualified traffic.

What I did

  • I went through six purchase journeys on desktop and mobile, screen by screen, and measured page speed (PageSpeed score of 67 on mobile, 79 on desktop).
  • Acquisition channels: SEO brings 42% of new users, direct traffic converts best (7.34%), social brings 13,392 visitors but converts at 1.44%. I also spotted a tracking error on the checkout and proposed a tagging plan.
  • On fast track: 2,366 add-to-carts for 640 purchases, a 73% drop-off. Likely causes: three clicks on “Continue”, a mandatory account before payment, a long form, a pop-up that cancels the booking, few payment options.
  • Hypotheses to validate with Hotjar (click, scroll and form heatmaps, session recordings) and a sized A/B test plan: 2,540 visitors per variant over 27 days.
  • SEO audit with Screaming Frog (275 links without anchor text, 69 meta descriptions too long, 12 images without alt text) and opportunity keywords: “parking aeroport nice”, 8,100 searches a month, the page ranks 28th.

Results

  • Nine prioritised recommendations, including a one-page checkout, guest checkout, account creation after payment and autofill with Google Pay and Apple Pay.
  • A ready-to-run A/B test plan and a page-by-page SEO roadmap (tags, structured data, H2s matched to search intent).
