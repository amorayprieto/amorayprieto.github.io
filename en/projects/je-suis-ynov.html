“Je suis Ynov”, a student testimonial video for social media

From pitch to final cut: brief, audience, production sheet, shoot and post-production.

  • 90 svertical format requested
  • 15 minof footage cut to 1 min 50
  • 2shooting days

Context

Sophia Ynov Campus's communication team wanted a series of short videos in which students share what they love about the school, for Instagram Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts. Audience: high-school students and people changing courses, aged 16 to 22, mainly in the Alpes-Maritimes.

What I did

  • Agency-style response to the brief: positioning, persona (Élise, 17, final year of high school), key message and tone.
  • Full pre-production: production sheet, synopsis, shot-by-shot shooting schedule, image release forms, interview script and quote.
  • Sound recording with a lavalier mic over two shooting days, and the on-camera testimonial.
  • Post-production: 15 minutes of footage cut to 1 min 50, 1080 × 1920, subtitles and voice-over.

Results

  • A vertical video ready to publish, delivered despite very short shooting and editing time.
  • A complete production file, reusable for the next episodes of the series.
Role
Sound engineer, and on-screen interviewee
