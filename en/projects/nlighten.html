Building nLighten's name in the Spanish market

Press relations, events, content and lead generation for Spain, paid campaigns for Europe.

  • Millionsreaders reached through Spanish media
  • 4events run in Spain in 2026
  • 7 payscovered by paid media campaigns
  • 3 languesFrench, English, Spanish every day

Context

nLighten runs around 35 edge data centers in seven European countries. In Spain the brand is new: it has to become known to network operators, enterprises, investors and public authorities, and bring qualified contacts to the sales team.

I run digital marketing and communication for the Spanish market, and paid campaigns across Europe. Leads land in HubSpot, then move to Salesforce once qualified.

What I did

  • Press relations, with the local agency: press releases (CEO appointment, nConnect launch in Spain, ReadyCabinet, a sovereign cloud proof of concept with Arqit), an op-ed in Ecoconstrucción, interviews with the Spanish management on energy and data center regulation.
  • Events: The Wave Zaragoza, DCD Invest Madrid, ESNOG-35 and a customer event at our Madrid data center. Content preparation, on-site presence, then contact qualification.
  • GEO: follow-up of an audit of nLighten's visibility in AI answer engines, to fix sources and make brand information more accurate.
  • Multilingual content FR / EN / ES: website, LinkedIn, press releases, SEO and AEO-optimised articles.
  • Demand generation: web forms connected to HubSpot and Salesforce, sponsored LinkedIn campaigns, funnel tracking up to the sales hand-off.
  • Paid media Europe: LinkedIn Ads, Google Ads and Microsoft Ads campaigns across the seven countries.

Results

  • Regular coverage in Spanish business and trade media, including El Economista, El Plural, IT User and Data Center Market, for a combined audience of several million readers.
  • nLighten now speaks as an industry voice on energy, digital sovereignty and data center regulation.
  • Better-qualified inbound leads and LinkedIn campaigns above B2B benchmarks.
  • Event conversations that lead to data center visits with enterprise accounts.
