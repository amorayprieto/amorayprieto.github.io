nLighten › Spain

Launching a data center brand in the Spanish market

Events, PR, content and demand gen for Spain, paid campaigns for Europe. Q2 2026 review.

  • 92 373people reached through press coverage
  • 24 930 €advertising value equivalent (AVE)
  • 50 %of web leads qualified as MQL
  • 2,25 %click-through rate on LinkedIn

Context

nLighten runs around 35 edge data centers in seven European countries. In Spain the brand is new: it has to become known to network operators, enterprises and investors, and bring qualified contacts to the sales team.

I run digital marketing and communication for the Spanish market, and paid campaigns across Europe. Leads land in HubSpot, then move to Salesforce once qualified.

What I did

  • Events: presence at three industry events, The Wave Zaragoza, DCD Invest Madrid and ESNOG-35. Content preparation, contact collection and qualification, and the data center visits that follow.
  • Press relations, with the local agency: three press releases (CEO appointment, nConnect launch in Spain, ReadyCabinet) and an op-ed published in Ecoconstrucción.
  • Content: updating and translating website and social content for the Spanish audience, in French, English and Spanish.
  • Demand generation: web forms connected to HubSpot and Salesforce, a sponsored LinkedIn post, tracking the lead → MAL → MQL → SAL → SQL funnel.
  • Paid media Europe: LinkedIn Ads, Google Ads and Microsoft Ads campaigns across the seven countries.

Results

  • 8 articles in trade media (EjePrime, BYTE TI, IT User, Data Center Market). All Spanish coverage for the first half of the year came from this quarter.
  • Half of the inbound website leads were qualified as MQLs.
  • A sponsored LinkedIn post with 4,000+ impressions and a 2.25% click-through rate, a strong level for B2B.
  • 80+ event contacts being qualified, including two enterprise leads.
