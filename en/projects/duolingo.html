Inbound marketing: Duolingo against Memrise, Babbel and Rosetta Stone

Benchmark of four language apps, personas, white paper reviews and AI-produced content.

  • 4apps benchmarked
  • 4personas built
  • 5content formats produced with AI

Context

Four language-learning apps, four business models. The brief was to understand their audiences, identify user needs, then design and produce inbound content that meets them.

What I did

  • Website audience analysis (traffic, age, country, top pages and articles) and Instagram analysis: best engagement slot, growth spike tied to the “death of Duo” campaign.
  • Socio-demographic, psychological and behavioural segmentation, then one persona per brand with their needs.
  • Critical review of white papers that answer those needs: structure, sources, tone, what's missing.
  • AI-assisted content production, step by step: the “Speak Fast, Learn Smart” podcast, explainer videos, shorts, a quiz app and an audiobook.

Results

  • A repeatable method to go from a persona need to published content, with the right AI tool for each format.
  • Prototype content presented at the final review, including the podcast interface and the quiz app.
